# Town of Us mods Among Us
This lists major mods related to Town of Us. Town of Us is an Among Us mod based on Town of Salem.

## Town of Us
Developer: slushiegoose<br>
Status: Discontinued<br>
Latest Among Us version supported: 2021.6.30<br>
Github: https://github.com/slushiegoose/Town-Of-Us<br>
Successor: Town of Polus (polus.gg)<br>
Supported Among Us Platforms: Steam, Epic Games

Original repo. Discontinued in favour of new polus.gg mod.

## Town of Polus (polus.gg)
Developer: polus.gg<br>
Status: Discontinued<br>
Latest Among Us version supported: 2021.6.30<br>
Github: https://github.com/polusgg/plugin-gamemode-town-of-polus<br>
Successor: Town of Polus (mouthwash.gg)<br>
Supported Among Us Platforms: Steam, Epic Games, Android(Planned)

Roles mod used for polus.gg. Rewrite of Town of Us. Some roles missing. polus.gg got discontinued a month after release.
